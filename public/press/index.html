Bio — copy & paste

Wrestle With Jimmy — Austin's Weezer cover band. The Blue Album, matching tracksuits, and a room that sings every word. — one line

Wrestle With Jimmy is Austin's Weezer cover band: four guys in matching blue tracksuits playing the Blue Album front to back, deep cuts off Pinkerton, and the '90s and 2000s alt rock that followed. They've played Mohawk, Come and Take It Live, Radio/East, Brisketfest, and breweries across Central Texas. — fifty words

Wrestle With Jimmy is Austin's Weezer cover band — and, more to the point, a reliable way to get a room singing. The four-piece plays the Blue Album front to back, pulls deep cuts off Pinkerton, and rounds out the night with the alt rock bangers the same crowd grew up on: the songs that made a generation of kids feel like it was okay to be a little weird.

Since forming in 2024 they've become a fixture on the Austin tribute circuit, playing Mohawk, Come and Take It Live, Radio/East, The Far Out Lounge and Hole in the Wall, plus brewery stages including Independence, Meanwhile, Central Machine Works and Still Austin Whiskey Co. Festival and anniversary bills include Brisketfest and Independence Brewing's 21st. They travel well: matching Adidas tracksuits, a fast load-in, and an unconditional love for Weezer. — one hundred fifty words
